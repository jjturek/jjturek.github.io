Moose MCP

A private connector between an AI assistant and one person's own Google account.

What it does

Moose MCP lets an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) work in the Google account of the person who authorised it, on that person's instruction. Through it the assistant can:

  • read, create and edit that person's Google Docs, Sheets and Slides;
  • find, organise and comment on files in that person's Google Drive;
  • write email drafts in that person's Gmail, for the person to review and send;
  • add events to that person's Google Calendar.

It runs on the user's own computer. It is not a public service, has no sign-up, and is used by its owner only.
